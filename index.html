ABOUT THE OFFICE

Department of Agrarian Reform Pangasinan

The Provincial Agrarian Reform Office serves as a field-level arm of the Department of Agrarian Reform in implementing agrarian reform programs and services throughout Pangasinan.

Our Mandate

The Department of Agrarian Reform is the lead government agency responsible for implementing comprehensive and genuine agrarian reform.

Its work focuses on equitable land distribution, land tenure security, agricultural productivity, and improving the quality of life of farmers and Agrarian Reform Beneficiaries.

Vision

An empowered and productive agrarian reform community enjoying secure land tenure and sustainable development.

Mission

To implement agrarian reform programs and provide responsive services to farmers and beneficiaries.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How Can We Help?

The Department of Agrarian Reform is the lead government agency responsible for implementing comprehensive and genuine agrarian reform in the Philippines.

Qualified Agrarian Reform Beneficiaries, farmers, farmer organizations, landowners, and other stakeholders may access applicable DAR programs and services subject to existing laws, rules, and regulations.

You may use the contact information provided on this website or visit the appropriate DAR provincial office for assistance.

Requests should be submitted through the appropriate DAR office or division depending on the nature of the concern.
